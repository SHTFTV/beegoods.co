The next little buzz: why BeeGoods exists

BeeGoods is the goods-focused next chapter beside Med Bee Tate. Here is what we are building, and what we want to get right.

BeeGoods Editorial · October 17, 2026

A small idea with room to grow

A quiet place can leave you wanting to take a little of its feeling home. That is the starting point for BeeGoods: bee-inspired goods with a clear story, useful details and an identity of their own. We are beginning with honey, wax, gifts and everyday objects as collection directions. Those are areas to explore, not a promise that every item pictured is already on a shelf.

Let the details do the talking

Before a product becomes part of a collection, the practical questions matter. Who made it? What is it made from? What size is it? What does it cost, and when can it be supplied? Beautiful imagery helps introduce a mood, but it cannot answer those questions on its own. We want a product page to give you enough information to decide whether something fits your home, your table or the person receiving it.

Follow the next chapter

BeeGoods sits beside Med Bee Tate within the wider Industry Army Marketing portfolio. The brands share a bee-inspired thread, while doing different jobs. Med Bee Tate develops the place and experience; BeeGoods develops the goods direction. Explore the collection pages, meet the connected brands and send a launch enquiry if you would like to hear more. There is no checkout or automatic mailing-list enrolment at this stage.
