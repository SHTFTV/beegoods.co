What we want to know about a handmade soap

Useful product information helps a maker’s work speak for itself.

BeeGoods Editorial · October 17, 2026

Tell us about the exact product

A soap introduction should name the product, its intended use, the maker and the full product information the maker supplies. “Handmade” explains part of the process; it does not tell a visitor everything in the bar or whether it suits their preferences. Send details for the actual item you would like considered rather than a broad description of everything your studio might make.

Make the visual honest

Photograph the product clearly and distinguish finished goods from work in progress. Include a view that makes the packaging and size understandable. If a photograph shows several scents, colours or sizes, explain which variants it represents. Avoid adding performance or treatment claims through captions that the product information does not support.

A conversation before a collection

BeeGoods is exploring natural-goods connections around its bee-inspired starting point. A maker introduction is an enquiry, not an acceptance or an order. Tell us what you make, the information available for each product and the practical supply details. We can then discuss whether it fits the developing collection without presenting an unconfirmed partnership as a finished collaboration.
