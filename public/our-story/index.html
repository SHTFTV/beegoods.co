The thread between the brands

A health and wellness portal can reveal ideas that need a home of their own. Med Bee Tate develops a bee-inspired greenhouse and rest-space concept. BeeGoods carries the goods direction into everyday life.

A different job for each brand

Treatments.tv provides the wider health and wellness context. Med Bee Tate explores a place to pause. BeeGoods explores objects for the table, the home and thoughtful giving. Industry Army Marketing connects the creative and digital work.

Still building

This is the start of the BeeGoods story. We are developing a collection, inviting useful maker conversations and keeping the stage of each idea clear. Our artwork and stock footage are labelled; product availability will be confirmed before anything is offered for sale.
